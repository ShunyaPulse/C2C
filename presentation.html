Phase 1 — Problem Framing (1.5 min) [Rubric D1: 5 pts]

Framing, Domain Shift & Asymmetric Error Costs

🎯 Field Reality & Asymmetry
• State missions have limited door-to-door capacity (only top ~20% households visited).

• Cost of False Negative: A vulnerable rural mother with respiratory distress is missed and remains reliant on firewood smoke.

• Cost of False Positive: A field worker visits a wealthy household that already uses an induction cooktop.
⚠️ Anticipated Domain Shift
• Hidden Test Set: Drawn from unseen districts not present in the training data.

• The Trap: Tree models memorize local district codes or raw geographic clusters.

• Our Defense: Dropped high-cardinality district labels; generalized on universal socio-economic proxies (structure pucca/kachha, electricity expenditure, appliance footprints).
🔒 Sandbox Constraints
• Execution Budget: 2 CPU cores, 4 GB RAM, strictly offline, ≤ 5 min runtime.

• File Rules: Zero data files > 5 MB committed in Git.

• Inference Speed: Our blended model evaluates 1,000 households in 3.8 seconds on 2 CPUs with zero GPU dependency.
Key Mathematical KPI Alignment: Precision@20% = 0.9583 ensures that among the top quintile selected for field dispatch, 96 out of 100 households genuinely cook on chulha, maximizing government subsidy efficacy.
Phase 1 — Data Engineering & Robustness

Non-Response Sanitization & Missingness Routing

🧹 The 88 / 99 / 9999 Code Trap
• Survey protocols code 88 (Refused), 99 (Don't know), and 9999 (Not Applicable).

• Naive Cleaning Flaw: Blind global replacement corrupts continuous monetary variables (e.g., electricity bills of ₹88 or ₹99/month, which represent ultra-poor lifelines).

• Targeted Engine: Implemented a 39-column dictionary-mapped sanitization pass that maps survey refusal codes to NaN while protecting genuine continuous quantities.
🌲 Categorical Missing Routing (-1 → NaN)
• Pandas pd.Categorical.codes maps unseen states or missing categories to -1.

• In standard XGBoost trees, -1 gets numerically bucketed alongside index 0 (e.g., Andhra Pradesh), creating artificial geographic bias.

• The Fix: Explicitly converted code -1 to np.nan, allowing gradient boosted trees to route missing survey branches natively into the learned optimal split.
Asset Proxies
Fridge ownership (q466) and TV screen size (q463) act as true wealth proxies over volatile stated income.
Infrastructure
Grid tenure (q311_grid_yrs) reveals generational grid stability vs. peripheral off-grid communities.
Dwelling Quality

Phase 2 — Features & Architecture [Rubric D2: 8 pts]

Calibrated Blended Ensemble Architecture

🤖 Model 1: XGBoost (Histogram Tree Method)
• Hyperparameters: max_depth=5, learning_rate=0.04, subsample=0.85
• Regularization: Explicit reg_alpha=0.5 (L1) and reg_lambda=2.0 (L2) to prevent memorizing rare survey combinations.
• Missing Handling: Default NaN direction routing.
• Single Model CV AUC: 0.9168
⚡ Model 2: LightGBM (Leaf-Wise Optimization)
• Hyperparameters: num_leaves=31, learning_rate=0.035, colsample_bytree=0.85
• Regularization: min_child_samples=30 to restrict leaf isolation.
• Diversity: Leaf-wise splitting explores asymmetric interaction patterns missed by depth-wise trees.
• Single Model CV AUC: 0.9175
🎯 Calibrated Champion Blend Formulation
P(Solid Fuel) = 0.55 × P_XGBoost + 0.45 × P_LightGBM
Probabilities strictly bounded in [1e-4, 1 - 1e-4] & rounded to 6 decimals.
Ensemble Gain: ROC-AUC jumps from 0.8865 (Baseline Logistic Regression) to 0.9201 (Blend), reducing Log Loss by 27.8% (0.498 → 0.359).

Intervention Priority Index (IPI) & Decision Dashboard

📐 The IPI Mathematical Formulation
Raw probability alone ignores human vulnerability. We engineered the Intervention Priority Index (IPI):
IPI = P(Solid) × [ 70 + 30 × VulnerabilityFactor ]

Where VulnerabilityFactor incorporates:
• Children < 18 yrs in household (respiratory impact)
• Elderly members ≥ 60 yrs
• Kachha wall structure (poor kitchen ventilation)

Phase 3 — Depth & Error Analysis [Rubric C1 & D3: 17 pts]

Fairness Slices Across 21 States & Failure Modes

📊 State Fairness Slices (docs/error_analysis.md)
• High Performing States: Uttar Pradesh (0.932 AUC), Bihar (0.928 AUC), Madhya Pradesh (0.925 AUC) — strong socio-economic signal separation.

• Challenging State: Kerala (0.871 AUC) — high baseline electrification and universal pucca housing, requiring deeper appliance granularity.

• Urban vs. Rural Slice: Rural AUC: 0.918 | Urban AUC: 0.924 (well-balanced calibration across sectors).
🔍 Identified Failure Modes ("Fuel Stacking")
• The False Positive Hazard: Households with large agricultural holdings generate free crop residue and biomass, continuing chulha use for livestock feed boiling despite owning an LPG cylinder.

• Income Underreporting: Informal agricultural workers report ₹0 or ₹2,000 monthly income, but high electricity bills expose actual economic capacity.

• Remedy: Prioritized durable physical assets (refrigerator, smartphone count) over reported income.
Honest Negative Result: Adding target-encoded high-cardinality district IDs produced an artificial +0.015 leap in local validation, but failed catastrophically when evaluated on unseen test districts. We ruthlessly rejected district target encoding in favor of pure invariant asset proxies.
